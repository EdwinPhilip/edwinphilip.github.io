## Hello, I'm Edwin

I'm a **DevOps and Platform Engineering Lead** with **8+ years of experience** across infrastructure engineering, cloud-native platforms, CI/CD and production operations. I currently lead a team of five engineers at **HackerRank**, where I combine technical direction with hands-on engineering.

My work sits at the intersection of **developer experience, reliability and platform architecture**. I enjoy taking complex operational problems and turning them into repeatable systems that help teams focus on building products.

### Selected outcomes

| Area | Impact |
| --- | --- |
| Faster engineering feedback | Helped reduce a Rails build-and-deployment workflow from approximately **45 minutes to 15 minutes** |
| Cloud platform operations | Worked across an estate of **15+ production and non-production Kubernetes clusters** |
| Developer self-service | Built reusable infrastructure, delivery and development-environment patterns |
| Operational knowledge | Designed and shipped **Kron**, an AI-assisted internal DevOps support platform |
| Team leadership | Lead and mentor a **five-engineer DevOps team**, remaining hands-on in design and operations |

### My career journey

I started in DevOps engineering at **Zensar Technologies**, developing a foundation in release automation, infrastructure and Kubernetes. At **HackerRank**, I grew from Senior DevOps Engineer to Lead DevOps Engineer, expanding my scope from implementation to platform strategy, mentoring and cross-team reliability.

I hold **CKA** and **CKAD** certifications and have an academic background in **Aeronautical Engineering**, followed by advanced computing studies at **C-DAC**. That mix of systems thinking and software engineering continues to shape how I approach platform work.
