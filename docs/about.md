## What I work on

### Platforms that developers enjoy using

A strong platform should remove repetitive work without hiding important engineering decisions. I focus on reusable workflows, clear interfaces, sensible defaults and safe self-service.

### Reliability as a product capability

I care about more than dashboards and uptime numbers. Good reliability work connects the user journey to measurable service health, incident learning and pragmatic architecture choices.

### Technical leadership with implementation depth

I enjoy mentoring, design reviews and cross-team collaboration. I also believe platform leaders should remain close enough to production systems to validate assumptions and understand operational trade-offs.
