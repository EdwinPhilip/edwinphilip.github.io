# Engineering Knowledge Base

A growing collection of practical engineering study guides, written for engineers who want to understand how systems behave in production.

The material is **public and vendor-aware**, with first principles, diagrams, real commands, failure scenarios, and challenging interview questions. Examples are hypothetical unless explicitly identified otherwise.

## Available

### [Networking Fundamentals — Complete Module 1](/guides/networking/)

Eight chapters covering browser-to-backend requests, TCP, DNS, TLS, load balancing, CIDR/NAT, Kubernetes/GKE networking, and production incident troubleshooting.
